## 📖 Story Overview

**Title**: The Last Guardian  
**Genre**: Fantasy Adventure  
**Scenes**: 5 (40 seconds total at 8 seconds each)  
**Characters**: 2 (Aria - Protagonist, Elder Theron - Mentor)  
**Locations**: 3 (Ancient Forest, Sacred Temple, Mountain Peak)

1. **Scene 1**: Aria begins her quest (Forest)
2. **Scene 2**: Aria meets Elder Theron (Temple)

5. **Scene 5**: Aria returns victorious (Temple)

## 🎬 Complete Scene Prompts

### **Scene 1: The Quest Begins**

**Location**: Ancient Forest  
**Characters**: Aria  
**Duration**: 8 seconds  
**Purpose**: Introduce protagonist and establish quest

#### **Full Prompt**
```
Wide shot. Aria, a woman in her late 20s, with a clear, determined 
alto voice, steady and purposeful tone, soft Irish accent, speaks 
with quiet courage and underlying vulnerability, as if carrying a 
great burden. She stands at the edge of a dark, ancient forest, 
morning mist swirling around her feet. She wears simple leather 
traveling clothes and carries a worn sword at her side. Her hand 
rests on the hilt as she looks toward the shadowy path ahead with 
resolve. She says: "They say no one returns from this forest. But I 
have no choice. The fate of my people depends on what lies within." 
Close-mic, clean audio, warm tone, no reverb. Ambient sound: rustling 
leaves, distant bird calls, wind through ancient branches. Dappled 
sunlight filtering through ancient trees, misty atmosphere, cinematic 
lighting. No subtitles.
```

**Word Count**: ~130 words  
**Key Elements**:
- ✅ Voice Anchor Block present
- ✅ Audio Environment specified
- ✅ Ambient Sound matches location
- ✅ Dialogue establishes quest
- ✅ Action shows determination
- ✅ No scene references

---

### **Scene 2: Meeting the Mentor**

**Location**: Sacred Temple  
**Characters**: Aria, Elder Theron  
**Duration**: 8 seconds  
**Purpose**: Introduce mentor, establish relationship

#### **Full Prompt**
```
Medium two-shot. Aria, a woman in her late 20s, with a clear, 
determined alto voice, steady and purposeful tone, soft Irish accent, 
speaks with quiet courage and underlying vulnerability, as if carrying 
a great burden. She stands on the left, looking up with a mixture of 
hope and uncertainty. Elder Theron, a man in his early 70s, with a 
deep, weathered voice, slow and contemplative tone, refined British 
accent, speaks with ancient wisdom and gentle patience, pausing 
thoughtfully between phrases. He stands on the right, wearing simple 
brown robes, holding an ancient wooden staff. Aria says: "Elder 
Theron, I seek the Crystal of Light. Can you help me find it?" Theron 
responds: "The crystal does not reveal itself to those who seek it... 
only to those who are worthy." Close-mic, clean audio, warm tone, no 
reverb. Ambient sound: echoing footsteps, distant chanting, stone 
acoustics. Torch-lit temple interior, dramatic shadows, ancient stone 
pillars. No subtitles.
```

**Word Count**: ~165 words  
**Key Elements**:
- ✅ BOTH Voice Anchor Blocks present
- ✅ Audio Environment specified (same for both)
- ✅ Ambient Sound matches location (Temple)
- ✅ Dialogue establishes mentor-student relationship
- ✅ Two-shot composition for dialogue
- ✅ No scene references

## 🎯 Why This Works

### **Voice Consistency Achieved Through:**

1. **Exact Repetition**: Voice Anchor Blocks are copied word-for-word
2. **Audio Lock**: Same audio environment for each character
3. **Location Lock**: Same ambient sound per location
4. **No References**: Each prompt is 100% standalone

### **Story Progression Achieved Through:**

1. **Varied Shot Types**: Wide → Two-shot → Close-up → Wide → Medium
2. **Emotional Arc**: Determined → Hopeful → Fearful → Triumphant
3. **Dialogue Evolution**: Quest → Seeking → Guidance → Trial → Victory
4. **Location Changes**: Forest → Temple → Mountain → Temple
5. **Character Interactions**: Solo → Dialogue → Monologue → Solo → Dialogue
